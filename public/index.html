What we test

Six domains of reasoning

No syllabus to memorize. We test how you think, not what you have been taught.

01
"What must be true?"

Deductive reasoning. Starting from facts or rules and working out what necessarily follows.

02
"What is likely to be true?"

Probabilistic reasoning. Weighing evidence and base rates, and updating sensibly as new information arrives.

03
"What causes what?"

Causal reasoning. Telling real causes from coincidence and spotting confounding variables.

04
"What should I do when others are thinking too?"

Strategic reasoning. Working out how others will act, and choosing accordingly.

05
"Is this argument sound?"

Critical reading. Finding hidden assumptions and gaps, and telling a valid case from one that only seems right.

06
"How do I structure this messy problem?"

Modelling & estimation. Breaking a vague problem into the parts that matter and reaching a defensible estimate.

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers — the full FAQ covers every topic.

Reasoning is the foundation of clear thinking, connecting mathematics, language, science, and everyday decisions. GRC gives students the chance to challenge their reasoning skills beyond the typical curriculum.

All current high school and university students, split into Junior, Youth, and Senior age groups. Everyone receives the same problems; higher groups need more points to qualify.

Everyone submits their own solutions. In the Qualification Round you may work with friends and classmates; the Semi-Final and Final Round must be completed alone.

Prizes and awards are worth a total of $1,500, alongside international and national recognition and certificates for every participant.
