General Information

Reasoning is the foundation of clear thinking, connecting mathematics, language, science, and everyday decisions. GRC gives students a chance to test their reasoning skills beyond the typical classroom curriculum, across deductive, probabilistic, causal, and strategic reasoning, critical reading, and modeling & estimation.

GRC does not aim to replace established subject olympiads. It is a supplementary program focused on accessibility: no fixed syllabus, digital participation from anywhere, a real-world research component, difficulty adjusted by age group, free tools for teachers, and an alumni ambassador network.

GRC is an independent educational program with a globally distributed volunteer and professional team. It is funded by sponsors and partners, together with the Semi-Final Round registration fee; financial aid covers participants who cannot afford that fee.

Eligibility

Any current high school or university student, worldwide. Participants are split into Junior, Youth, and Senior age groups — see Age Groups for the exact cutoffs.

Everyone submits their own solutions. In the Qualification Round you may work with friends or classmates; the Semi-Final and Final Round must be completed entirely on your own.

Yes. Returning participants are welcome and often place higher the second time around.

Prizes and Registration

Prizes and awards are worth a total of $1,500 USD, alongside international and national recognition and a certificate for every participant.

Yes. Every participant receives a digital certificate of participation regardless of their result, with a permanent verification ID.

The Qualification Round is free. The Semi-Final Round has a small registration fee to cover evaluation and infrastructure; financial aid, including full waivers, is available.

Teachers, Schools, and Ambassadors

Yes. Schools participate automatically once a student registers under their name, and schools with strong participation and performance receive end-of-year recognition. Supervising teachers receive a certificate for their role.

Ambassadors get posters and flyers, training materials, outreach ideas, and a personal GRC email address. See Become an Ambassador for the full benefits and levels.
