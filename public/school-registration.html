School Registration

Register your school as an official GRC participating institution to run group sign-ups, track student results, and unlock reduced Semi-Final fees.

Who should register

Any school, from any country, that wants to offer the Global Reasoning Challenge to its students as an extracurricular activity. One coordinator (usually a teacher or academic staff member) registers on behalf of the school.

Registering a school is separate from an individual teacher account — the school profile lets you manage multiple teachers and cohorts of students under one roof.

What you get

A school dashboard listing every registered student and their round-by-round results, bulk CSV import for student rosters, discounted Semi-Final registration fees for groups of 10 or more, and eligibility for School Awards.

Steps

How to register your school

After submitting, your coordinator account is reviewed within two business days. Once approved, you can invite additional teachers and import your student roster. See School Partnerships for the full benefits of a deeper partnership.
