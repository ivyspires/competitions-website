Teacher Registration

Register as a teacher to supervise exams, follow your students' results, and bring GRC into your classroom as an extracurricular activity.

What a teacher account gives you

  • View your students' results, performance reports, and certificates in one dashboard.
  • Option to cover Semi-Final Round registration costs for your group.
  • Ability to supervise the Semi-Final Round exam in person, as an alternative to the online supervision system.
  • A certificate recognizing your role, issued after the Semi-Final Round.

Register as a teacher

We manually verify each teacher account within a few business days.

Supervising the Semi-Final Round

To supervise a student's exam, you must be currently employed at a government-accredited school or university, currently active (not retired), and not related to the participant. During the exam, you take a single photo of the exam conditions as confirmation.

If no eligible teacher is available, students can instead use the online supervision system described on the Rounds & Process page.
