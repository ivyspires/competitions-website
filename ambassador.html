Local Ambassador Program

Become an Ambassador

Coordinate a local group, run outreach activities, and represent GRC in your community while building real leadership experience.

Mission

  • Promote reasoning & critical thinking through local activities.
  • Build a local group of interested students.
  • Increase participation across your school or region.
  • Support participants with guidance and answers.

Benefits

  • Official GRC Ambassador Certificate.
  • Leadership & career experience.
  • Priority consideration for financial aid.
  • Recognition on the website & social media.
  • A personal GRC email address.

Requirements

  • At least 14 years old.
  • Motivated to encourage reasoning and problem-solving.
  • Basic English proficiency.
  • Two-stage application: a motivation letter, CV, and transcript, followed by a short introduction video and reasoning problems.
Growth path

Ambassador levels

Ambassadors advance through five recognized levels as their outreach grows.

Newcomer — application approved Experienced — 1+ year, 5+ participants Accomplished — 3+ Final Round participants Distinguished — 2+ years, outstanding events Honoured — received an ambassador award

Ongoing commitment

Ambassadors upload a short quarterly report on their outreach activities and are expected to keep their activity log up to date. Ambassadors who successfully encourage five or more students to reach the Semi-Final Round typically receive priority financial aid for their own registration costs.
