Financial Aid

The Qualification Round is always free. Financial aid, including full waivers, is available so the Semi-Final Round fee is never the reason a student sits out.

Why there is a fee at all

Running an international competition at this scale has real costs: evaluation, technical infrastructure, materials, and support for participants across more than 150 countries. Sponsors and partners cover part of this, and the Semi-Final Round registration fee covers the rest — there are no other required costs.

Financial aid exists specifically so this fee never becomes a barrier to participation.

What aid covers

Aid ranges from a partial reduction to a full waiver of the Semi-Final Round registration fee, based on socio-economic need. Ambassadors who have helped five or more students reach the Semi-Final Round typically receive priority consideration for their own registration.

There is no separate fee for the Qualification Round or the Final Round.

Request financial aid

Requests are reviewed confidentially and do not affect your Qualification Round result.
